# Logos

The STATIC LINE wordmark, separated out of the original artwork, plus the background plate it sat on. The wordmark is final and agreed by the whole band — never redraw, restyle or regenerate it.

| File | Use it for | Do not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `staticline-wordmark.svg` | Print and small sizes: stickers, shirts, favicon, anything that scales. One colour, takes `currentColor`. | Expect texture — the vector has none, by design. |
| `staticline-wordmark.png` | The web hero and large on-screen use. Carries the cracks, paint and lightning. | Use below about 400px wide; the detail turns to noise. |
| `staticline-wordmark-flat.png` | Small sizes on a dark ground: footer, nav, share images. | Place on a light ground. |
| `staticline-wordmark-black.png` | Small sizes on a light ground: printed forms, a light-background press kit. | Place on a dark ground. |
| `staticline-background.jpg` | Hero backgrounds and section fills. Splatter, brush strokes and lightning intact. | Put running text or form fields directly on it. |

## Placement

The wordmark goes large at the top of the homepage and small in the footer. It does not sit prominently on every page — that was a unanimous decision, and repeating it at full strength drains it.

Give it room: clear space on all sides of at least the cap height of the letters. It is a dense mark and it suffocates against other elements.

## Known detail

The background plate has a faint smear across the middle where the letters were removed. At full-page scale it reads as part of the grunge. If it ever shows, the plate can be rebuilt clean.
